# Data sources

Age-specific death rates (per 100,000) from liver cirrhosis, United States, both sexes, all races.

| Years | Source | Table |
|---|---|---|
| 1970–1999 | NIAAA Surveillance Report #93, *Liver Cirrhosis Mortality in the United States, 1970–2009* | Table 2 |
| 2000–2019 | NIAAA Surveillance Report #118, *Liver Cirrhosis Mortality in the United States: National, State, and Regional Trends, 2000–2019* | Table 2 |
| 2022–2023 | NCHS National Vital Statistics Reports Vol. 75 No. 8, *Deaths: Final Data for 2023* | Table 5 (chronic liver disease and cirrhosis) |

Notes
- 2020 and 2021 are not included.
- 1972 figures are based on a 50% sample of deaths (per NIAAA footnote).
- NIAAA and NCHS cause definitions are close but not identical; 2022–2023 values may not be perfectly comparable with earlier years.
- All data are U.S. government works in the public domain.
